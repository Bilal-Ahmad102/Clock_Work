ClockWork — Level 2: The Captain's Stage

Design document v1 (draft for review) — 2026-07-12 — follows the Intro Level (Undercroft combat tutorial). Purpose of the level: introduce the Audience and the Captain as living systems the player can feel.

1. Design goals

  • G1 — The enemy does not die, it recasts. Player must experience at least one on-stage form collapse followed by a recast into a fresh vessel, and understand the difference from a normal kill.
  • G2 — Someone is watching. The Audience is introduced visually (the Pit) and mechanically: player's accumulated style measurably changes the Captain's behavior mid-level.
  • G3 — Style has consequences. Brutal/repetitive play makes the vessel faster and more erratic (volatility up); varied/restrained play keeps favor alive. The level teaches this with one controlled contrast, not a lecture.
  • G4 — Continuity of tone. Prison-theater vocabulary continues from the intro level (Yard, Prompter's Cell, Fly Loft): this level is the stage house itself.

Non-goals: no magic (still sealed), no true_death ending in normal play (the level ends before recasts run out — true_death is reserved for a later level), no meta-progression UI beyond the two new bars.

2. Narrative framing

Marla leaves the Undercroft through the eastern exit and enters the backstage of the theater proper. The play never stops here: The Captain is a role, not a person — bodies are conscripted into it and the Audience insists the show continues. Marla interrupts a performance. The role binds to a vessel, she cuts it down, and the house does not let the curtain fall: the role limps into the next understudy in front of her. The level ends with the scarred Captain retreating deeper into the theater — favor bleeding, poise cracked — setting up the true confrontation later.

3. Level structure — five beats

Beat 1 — STAGE DOOR (arrival, ~60s). Quiet corridor + prop storage. Movement only. Dialogue Manager monologue #1 (Marla, portrait balloon): she hears applause through the walls. One poster prop: "THE CAPTAIN — EVERY NIGHT, FOREVER." Checkpoint 1.
Beat 2 — THE WINGS (foreshadow, ~90s). Vertical backstage: ropes, counterweights, reuse of grab-edge climbing. Through gaps in the curtain the player sees the Pit — rows of silhouette props (the Audience) facing an empty stage. Balloon line from offstage: the Prompter feeding lines. Optional secret: catwalk above with a cog + shrine (mirrors intro-level Fly Loft pattern). Checkpoint 2.
Beat 3 — FIRST BLOOD (first fight, ~2 min). Marla steps onto the stage; camera limit region locks to the proscenium (reuse camera_limit_zone). Captain.bind_role(&"the_captain", [vessel_1, vessel_2, vessel_3]) fires; Vessel 1 (thread_puppet) walks on. Favor + Poise bars fade in (new UI). Fight to Form 0 → form collapse on stage: vessel drops, Audience noise swells, scar applies (poise −15, integrity −10, volatility +20), and the role recasts into Vessel 2 which stands up from the front row of the Pit. Monologue #2: Marla realizes killing the body did nothing. Checkpoint 3 (mid-fight checkpoint restores at stage edge, Captain metrics persist — the Audience remembers).
Beat 4 — THE TURN (taught contrast, ~2 min). Vessel 2 fight. Volatility from the Beat-3 scar is now visible: +10% move speed, tighter/jittery attack pacing, higher heavy-attack chance (numbers in §6). Audience pressure loop is live: if the player spams one attack, favor visibly drains and the Prompter heckles ("They've seen this trick"); if the player varies verbs or parries, a restraint line plays instead. One scripted parry prompt: successful parry triggers Captain.damage_back_attacking_vessel() — the crowd gasps. Fight ends at Form 0 → second scar → recast begins…
Beat 5 — CURTAIN (exit, ~45s). …but this time the recast is staged: Vessel 3 catches the role, staggers, and withdraws through the trapdoor rather than fighting (favor too low to risk a third collapse tonight). Monologue #3: the Audience turns, as one, to look at Marla. Exit door to Level 3 unlocks. End-of-level state: recast_count = 2, volatility 40, scarred poise/integrity carried forward by the Audience's memory design.

8. Open questions (for review)

  • Should Audience patterns (brutality/precision/…) persist from the intro level's training dummies? Current rule: intro is Audience-free — proposal: Level 2 starts the memory at zero, first "real" performance.
  • Favor/Poise bars always visible during fights, or only when the value moves (juice pulse like the player HP bars)?
  • Vessel visual differentiation: palette swap of thread_puppet per vessel, or 3 distinct silhouettes? Blockout can ship with palette swap.
  • Does checkpoint death during Beat 3/4 reset Captain metrics? Proposal: no — Audience remembers across Marla's deaths too (reinforces the fiction, softens difficulty via scars persisting).
  • Pit safe zone: allow the player to walk among the audience silhouettes, or fence the stage? Proposal: allow it once, silhouettes never react — unsettling beat, zero cost.
